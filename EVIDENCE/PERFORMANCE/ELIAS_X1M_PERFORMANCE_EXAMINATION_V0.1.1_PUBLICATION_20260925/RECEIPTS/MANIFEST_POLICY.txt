# Final SHA-256 Manifest Policy

SHA256SUMS.txt contains the SHA-256 identity of every regular file in this evidence package except:

- SHA256SUMS.txt
- SHA256SUMS.sha256

The manifest cannot hash itself without recursion.

SHA256SUMS.sha256 is the sidecar SHA-256 identity of SHA256SUMS.txt.

Any post-freeze modification to a manifested file invalidates the manifest and requires a new evidence version rather than silent repair.

The manifest is the content-freeze boundary for this package.

Evidence stops where the evidence stops.
