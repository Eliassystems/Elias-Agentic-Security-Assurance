# Measurement History and Corrections

The examination deliberately preserved measurements that did not
survive stronger repeatability testing.

## Early observations

Earlier per-call measurements produced materially different absolute
latency values under different measurement contexts.

An experimental V3 same-run examination observed approximately:

MEAN = 0.917 microseconds
P50  = 0.900 microseconds
P95  = 1.000 microseconds
P99  = 1.020 microseconds

That result was NOT treated as established.

## Fresh-process challenge

Fresh-process examination did not reproduce the sub-microsecond mean.

Accordingly:

SUB_MICROSECOND_REPEATABLE_CLAIM = NOT ESTABLISHED

The 0.917 microsecond result is retained only as a historical
context-sensitive observation and is not part of the release claim.

## Selected comparative protocol

The later ABBA batch protocol was used to reduce measurement-order and
per-call timer effects.

Authority construction was performed outside the timed region.

Ten fresh processes were examined using:

CURRENT -> V3 -> V3 -> CURRENT

The comparative improvement was materially more stable than earlier
absolute-latency observations.

This correction history is intentionally retained.

No retrospective repair has been applied to the earlier observations.
